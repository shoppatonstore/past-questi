# JAMB Physics 1989

**1. Which of the following is a set of vectors?**
- A. Force, mass and moment
- **B. Acceleration, velocity and moment ✓**
- C. Mass, weight and density
- D. Mass, volume and density

**Explanation:** Vectors have both magnitude and direction. Force, acceleration, velocity, and moment are vectors. Mass, weight (as a scalar measure), density, and volume are scalars. Option B contains only vector quantities.

---

**2. The magnitude of the resultant of two mutually perpendicular forces, F₁ and F₂ is 13N. If the magnitude of F₁ is 5N, what is the magnitude of F₂?**
- A. 2.6 N
- B. 8.0 N
- **C. 12.0 N ✓**
- D. 18.0 N

**Explanation:** For perpendicular forces: R² = F₁² + F₂². So 13² = 5² + F₂², giving 169 = 25 + F₂². Therefore F₂² = 144, and F₂ = 12 N.

**3. Two points on a velocity-time graph have coordinates (5s, 10ms⁻¹) and (20s, 20ms⁻¹). Calculate the mean acceleration between the two points.**
- A. 0.67 ms⁻²
- B. 0.83 ms⁻²
- C. 1.50 ms⁻²
- D. 2.00 ms⁻²

**Explanation:** Mean acceleration = Δv/Δt = (20-10)/(20-5) = 10/15 = 0.67 ms⁻². The answer is **A. 0.67 ms⁻² ✓**

**4. A simple pendulum of mass m moves along an arc of a circle radius R in a vertical plane. What is the work done by gravity in a downward swing through the angle θ to 0°?**
- A. mgR sin θ
- **B. mgR (1 - cos θ) ✓**
- C. mgR
- D. mgR (1 - sin θ)

**Explanation:** The vertical height dropped is h = R - R cos θ = R(1 - cos θ). Work done by gravity = mgh = mgR(1 - cos θ).

- A. Q
- B. R
- C. A point between P and R
- **D. A point between R and S ✓**

**Explanation:** When a portion is removed from a uniform object, the center of gravity shifts away from the removed section. Since the cut is made from the P side, the center shifts toward S.

**6. A block of mass m is held in equilibrium against a vertical wall by a horizontal force. If the coefficient of friction between the block and the wall is μ, the minimum value of the horizontal force is:**
- A. μmg
- B. (1 – μ)mg
- C. (1 + μ)mg
- **D. mg/μ ✓**

**Explanation:** For equilibrium: Friction force = Weight, so μF = mg, where F is the horizontal force. Therefore F = mg/μ.

- **A. -24.5°C ✓**

**13. A mass of gas at 7°C and 70cm of mercury has a volume of 1200cm³. Determine its volume at 27°C and pressure of 75cm of mercury.**
- A. 1200 cm³
- **B. 1378 cm³ ✓**

- D. 4629 cm³

**24. A far sighted person cannot see objects that are less than 100cm away. If this person wants to read a book at 25cm, what type and focal length of lens does he need?**
- A. Convex, 20 cm
- B. Concave, 20 cm
- **C. Convex, 33 cm ✓**
- D. Concave, 33 cm

**Explanation:** For far-sightedness (hypermetropia), a convex lens is needed. The lens must form a virtual image at the near point (100cm) when the object is at the desired reading distance (25cm). Using the lens formula with u = -25cm and v = -100cm: 1/f = 1/v - 1/u = 1/(-100) - 1/(-25) = -1/100 + 1/25 = 3/100. Therefore f = 33.3 cm ≈ 33 cm. The correct answer is **C. Convex, 33 cm**.
